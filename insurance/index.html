What the insurance covers

  • Vet treatment for new illnesses after the waiting period
  • Treatment for accidents and injuries, such as road accidents, falls, bites and swallowed objects
  • Surgery and hospital stays linked to a covered illness or accident
  • Diagnostic tests and medicines your vet prescribes for that treatment
  • Up to the cover amount you choose, within the policy's sub-limits

What it usually does not cover

  • Pre-existing conditions: anything your dog or cat had, or showed signs of, before the policy started
  • Claims in the waiting period: illness in the first weeks after you buy (the exact period is in the policy)
  • Routine and preventive care: vaccines, deworming, flea and tick treatment, grooming and health check-ups
  • Pregnancy, breeding and birth costs
  • Cosmetic procedures such as ear or tail cropping
  • Food, supplements and diet products, even if a vet suggests them
  • Injury from neglect, cruelty or fighting, or dogs used for racing, guarding or commercial work
  • Dental, behaviour and training costs unless the policy says otherwise

Good to know before you buy

Age limitsInsurers accept dogs and cats within an age range, often from a few months old up to around 8–10 years for a new policy. Your advisor confirms this for your breed.

Help

Pet insurance questions

Is pet insurance available in India?

Yes. Several general insurers in India offer insurance for dogs and cats. Cover, eligibility by age and breed, and waiting periods differ by insurer and policy.

What does dog insurance usually cover?

Policies typically cover vet treatment for illness and accidents, surgery and hospitalisation, within the limits and exclusions in the policy wording. Pre-existing conditions are usually not covered.

What do I need to get a quote?

Your dog or cat's breed, age and health history, your vet's details and your contact. Some insurers also ask for photos from five angles, a vaccination declaration, and a Kennel Club of India certificate for high cover amounts.

How long does a quote take?

Filling in the details takes about 3 minutes. A licensed advisor then sends your quote on WhatsApp.

Who provides the insurance?

A licensed insurer, arranged through an IRDAI-registered intermediary. Their names and registration numbers are shared with your quote before you buy.
